# 11: Creation methods by people

**What to build:** A team lead sees how boxes are really made: how many were drawn by hand, pasted and propagated, and how long each of those takes. See Creation method, Drawn, Pasted and Propagated in `GLOSSARY.md`, the spec (`../spec.md`) and the creation methods card in the prototype.

**Blocked by:** 09 (Bounding boxes).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Drawn, pasted and propagated boxes are counted separately for the scope.
- [ ] Each of the three has its own average time, measured by the same rule as every operation.
- [ ] One average is shown for all three together, and it is the figure the Creation operation uses.
- [ ] A card lists the methods with their counts, shares and average times, one bar per method.
- [ ] The headline average time per box shows the per-method averages beneath it.
- [ ] The figures respect the period, the shape switch and the project and annotator filters.
- [ ] Under All shapes the same split is shown for every kind of object.
